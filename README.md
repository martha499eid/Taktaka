# 📎 Taktaka (تكتكة)

## 📌 Project Overview

Taktaka (تكتكة) is a mobile application designed to make it easier and faster for users to find home maintenance service providers.

The idea behind Taktaka comes from a common problem: when people need a home service such as plumbing, electrical work, air conditioning maintenance, or other services, it can be difficult to find a suitable technician quickly. When a technician is found through random or informal sources, the service may be expensive, and there may be uncertainty about the quality and reliability of the technician.

Taktaka aims to solve this problem by providing a platform that connects customers with home service providers in one place, making it easier to find a suitable technician quickly and choose from different available service options at reasonable prices.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Make it easier for users to find suitable home service providers.
- Provide quick access to technicians when users need a home service.
- Bring different types of home services together in one application.
- Provide users with multiple service options to choose from.
- Help users find services at reasonable and suitable prices.
- Reduce the need to search randomly for technicians.
- Make the process of requesting and managing home services easier and more organized.
- Improve trust and transparency between customers and service providers.

---

## 📦 Project Scope

Taktaka will provide a range of home maintenance services and features, including:

### 🛠️ Home Services

- Plumbing services.
- Electrical services.
- Air conditioning services.
- Carpentry services.
- Other essential home maintenance services.

### 📱 Application Features

- User registration and login.
- Service categories.
- Browse available services.
- Search for specific services.
- View service provider information.
- Request home maintenance services.
- Manage service requests.
- Multiple service options and prices.
- Ratings and reviews for service providers.
- Customer profile management.
- Service provider profile management.
